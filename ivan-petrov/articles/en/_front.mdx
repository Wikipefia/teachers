# Ivan Petrov

Professor of Mathematics at the University, with 15 years of teaching experience. My research interests span linear algebra, matrix analysis, discrete mathematics, and their applications in computational science.

## Teaching Approach

I believe in teaching through geometric intuition first, formalism second. Before proving a theorem, I want students to *see* it, *feel* it, and *predict* it. A 3D visualization of eigenvalues teaches more than ten pages of proofs.

<Callout type="info">
**My motto**: "If you can't draw a picture of a concept, you probably don't understand it yet."
</Callout>

## Courses I Teach

- **Linear Algebra** (Semester 1) — Vector spaces, matrices, determinants, eigenvalues
- **Discrete Mathematics** (Semester 2) — Logic, combinatorics, graph theory, recurrences
