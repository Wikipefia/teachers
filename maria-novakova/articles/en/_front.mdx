# Maria Novakova

Associate Professor of Computer Science, specializing in algorithms, computational complexity, and programming education. I've been teaching for over a decade and believe in a **practical-first** approach.

## My Approach

<Callout type="info">
You learn programming by programming, not by reading about programming. Every concept in my courses is immediately followed by hands-on exercises.
</Callout>

I structure my courses around a simple loop:

1. **Demonstrate** — show a working solution to a real problem
2. **Deconstruct** — break it apart, explain each piece
3. **Practice** — students implement variations themselves
4. **Reflect** — discuss what worked, what didn't, and why

## Courses I Teach

- **Programming Fundamentals** (Semester 1) — Python, data structures, algorithmic thinking
- **Discrete Mathematics** (Semester 2) — Co-lecturer: graph algorithms and complexity

## Research

My research focuses on:
- Algorithm design for NP-hard problems
- Approximation algorithms with provable guarantees
- Computational complexity theory
- Evidence-based programming education
